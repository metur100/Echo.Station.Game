## Version 1.0

**Promotional text (170):**
`A damaged research ship, a crew woken too early, and a ship that remembers everything that happens to it. Repair it, explore, decide what it becomes.`

**Description:**

```
The research vessel ESV Meridian was sent past the edge of the charts to study a signal no one could explain. Seven months in, every system failed at once. Three of the crew woke early. The reactor is running hot, there is no oxygen plant, and behind a sealed storage door something is glowing.

You are ECHO, the ship's caretaker.

A LIVING SHIP
Build and upgrade 14 kinds of rooms on an isometric hull. Underneath runs a real simulation: power shared by priority, oxygen, food and water, heat moving through walls, wear, breaches. Inspect any room to see exactly why it works — or why it doesn't.

THE SHIP REMEMBERS
Reactor overloads leave residual energy in nearby rooms. Dead organisms leave contamination. Rooms damaged again and again develop structural fatigue. Big decisions are remembered. Echoes stay where they happened and change what happens next — and research can turn them into technology.

ANOMALIES THAT FOLLOW RULES
The Lumen Bloom, the Static Choir and the Void Frost grow and spread by rules you can read, including which room is at risk next. Quarantine, purge, send your crew, or learn to harness them.

A CREW WITH HISTORY
Skills, traits, health, morale, fatigue and memories shape how your crew repairs, researches, maintains, prepares expeditions and responds to emergencies.

EXPLORE AND DECIDE
Four research branches, four expedition destinations, incidents with real choices, and a campaign with three endings shaped by how your ship evolves.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Fully offline. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection. Pause any time or play at 2× and 4×; the ship keeps running safely while you are away. Three save slots, achievements, reduced motion and larger text.

A game by Medin Turkes.
```
